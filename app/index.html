For Artists

Everything GigsFill gives you as a working musician.
  • Find gigs in your area. Interactive calendar with city, distance, gig-type, and pay filters.
  • One-click booking. No back-and-forth emails. See an open slot, book it, you're done.
  • Media-rich profile. Bio, photos, audio, video, and links to Spotify, Instagram, YouTube, and more.
  • Get notified first. Become a venue's Preferred Artist and the open-gig blast hits your inbox before anyone else.
  • Digital contracts. Sign, countersign, and store every contract right on the gig.
  • Direct Stripe payouts. Money transfers begin automatically the day after each gig — no chasing checks.
  • Vanity URL. Get a clean link to share on socials, EPKs, and texts — gigsfill.com/yourname.
  • Calendar sync. One-click iCal export so booked gigs flow into Google or Apple Calendar.
  • Ratings & reviews. Build credibility from venues you've played — visible on your public profile.
  • Waitlist alerts. Join the waitlist for gigs you want to play and get pinged when the gig opens up due to a cancellation.
  • Multi-user accounts. Bandmates, agents, or anyone you want can log in to the same artist profile.
  • Mobile-friendly. Browse, book, and sign contracts from your phone.

For Venues

Everything GigsFill gives you to run live music at your venue.
  • Post gigs in seconds. Single-slot, multi-slot, or recurring weekly — all from one calendar view.
  • Auto-blast open gigs. Notify every qualified artist within a radius you choose — fill open nights fast.
  • Preferred Artists list. Curate the bands you love; create frequency limits, priority notifications, decide who gets first dibs.
  • Beautiful flyers, built in. Drag-and-drop flyer editor with templates — every gig gets a sharable poster.
  • Status-coded calendar. Open, booked, pending contract, cancelled — color-coded for easy viewing.
  • Digital contracts. Sign and countersign contracts on platform; digital contracts or PDF contracts stored for every gig.
  • Stripe payments built in. Credit card is stored in Stripe, automatically pay artists the day after a gig. You have option to cancel payment if artist is a no-show or for other reasons.
  • Multi-user accounts. Owners and managers each get their own login on one venue account to help manage gigs and calendar.
  • Real-time waitlist. When a booked artist cancels, GigsFill auto-contacts the next artist in line — no scrambling.
  • Ratings & reviews. Vet artists before you book them; see how they've performed at other venues.
  • Vanity URL. Get gigsfill.com/yourvenue for socials, Google listings, and posters.
  • Email Center. Send broadcast updates to artists on your preferred list — without leaving the app.
  • Mobile-friendly. All site functionality can be run from your phone.
