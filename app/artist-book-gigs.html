Member Availability

Read-only view of the personal blackout dates each band member has added on their own profile. These are soft warnings — bookings on these dates still go through, but the artist gets an alert like "Jim is unavailable that night — play as a duo?" so the band can decide. Members edit their own dates from My Profile → My Availability.

1099-K & Tax Documents

Because GigsFill processes your payments through Stripe Connect, Stripe issues your annual Form 1099-K directly — you don't get a separate 1099-NEC from each venue. Stripe generates it automatically when your yearly Connect payouts cross the IRS threshold ($2,500 for tax year 2026, dropping to $600 for 2027+), files it with the IRS, and posts a copy in your Stripe Tax Center.
💡 Tip: If you fill out the W-9 above before clicking "Connect to Stripe" in the Payments tab, GigsFill pre-fills your legal name, address, and TIN into Stripe's onboarding form so you don't type them again. To edit your info afterward, use the Stripe Tax Center below.
